  Summary
  On 10/20 at 12:12 JST, dsr-fullstack#103 (DSR-585) removed 9 fields from the dm_product
  CF model that 4 persisted queries and dsr-eds still read. AEM rejects a whole GraphQL
  query when it asks for a field missing from the schema, so the product lists, the product
  detail page and the search count stopped receiving data. The queries were fixed in
  commit 5e827682 on develop (13:25 JST). The front end was aligned by dsr-eds#189,
  merged at 13:50 JST.

  Status: resolved.



  Impact
  Four of the eleven persisted queries asked for removed fields. The video, catalog and
  product-search-count queries were not affected.

  Root cause
  The CF model was changed on its own, without updating the queries that read it. Yet the
  model and the persisted queries live in the same repository and the same ui.content
  package.

        No automated check. The Maven build does not validate persisted queries against the
        CF models. The error only shows up at runtime on AEM.
        No review. PR #103 was merged by its author 20 minutes after it was opened, with no
        reviewer and no description of the removed fields.
        Hidden front-end dependency. dsr-eds reads these fields from another repository.
        Nothing links a model change to the blocks that depend on it.


  Resolution
  The removed fields were dropped from the 4 queries, the new fields added to the 2 detail
  queries, then dsr-eds was cleaned up.

  dsr-fullstack, commit 5e827682

         dsr-product-list , dm-product-list : 8 fields removed.

         dsr-product-page : topBottomStopVariation removed. Added
         itemVariationsImage , colorVariationsImage , Howtouse , HowtouseImage ,
         recycledMaterial , recycledMaterialNote , parentProduct ,
         applicableSliderStandard and applicableSliderSpecial . The 4 product
        references share a ProductLink fragment.
         product-detail : 7 fields removed, same additions.

  dsr-eds, #189

        product-list: removed the tag groups, the plastic parts filter, the standard color card
        badge and the             生産国
                             tooltip row.
        product-detail and cf-templates/product.html : removed the stop variation. The new
        fields are parsed but not rendered yet, as there is no design for them.

  Verification: braces balanced and fields checked one by one against the develop model.
  The queries were not run in GraphiQL, and the pages were not checked in a browser.
